My emperor penguin research uses biologging data to examine diving behavior, movement, energetic expenditure, and prey capture attempts.

I integrate accelerometry, depth, GPS, and behavioral-state models to understand how penguins adjust their foraging strategies in a changing marine environment.

Read my published master's thesis [here](https://scholarworks.sjsu.edu/etd_theses/5746/) or [email me](mailto:taylorazizeh@gmail.com) for a copy.
