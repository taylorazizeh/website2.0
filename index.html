About my work

Connecting individual energetics to marine ecosystem change

My research focuses on how marine predators respond to environmental variability, energetic constraints, and human impacts.

I combine field observations, aerial photogrammetry, biologging, stable isotopes, movement data, and statistical modeling to study whales, penguins, and other marine wildlife.
