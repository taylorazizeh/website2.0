My gray whale research examines how environmental conditions, migration, reproduction, and human impacts influence individual energetic status and population health.

I use aerial photogrammetry, stable isotopes, movement data, long-term monitoring records, and quantitative models to investigate changes in body condition and their ecological consequences.
